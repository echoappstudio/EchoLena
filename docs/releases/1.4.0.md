## EchoLena 1.4.0

Clipboard Shelf is now **EchoLena**, with a new look.

- A new icon: a silver clipboard with black lines on a black tile.
- **Signature**, a black, charcoal and silver look, is the default, light or dark to match your Mac. The shelf has a solid background, silver highlights and a lit top edge in dark mode.
- Six skins, each with a light and a dark version: Signature, Sky, Green, Turquoise, Bordeaux and Wisteria. Choose one from **⋯ → Skin**; the app's icon in Finder follows it.
- A new menu-bar picture: a solid clipboard, with pause bars inside while saving is paused.
- Pinned items are marked with the skin's own accent colour (silver in Signature) instead of yellow.

This is a fresh install, not an update to the old app. Download `EchoLena-1.4.0.zip`, drag EchoLena into Applications, and delete the old copy first. Your old history, pins and settings are not carried over.
